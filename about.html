This site

The Hub lists 37 hand-picked publications, tools and methods, and innovation catalogues on climate from across CGIAR, each linked to where it is published. A search also covers the 300 most recent open-access journal articles, working papers, reports and briefs filed under CGIAR’s climate adaptation and mitigation impact area on CGSpace, a list that is refreshed from CGSpace once someone has reviewed the changes. Climate data, and the related data used in adaptation, mitigation and risk assessment, are on the Climate Data Hub.

Climate at CGIAR

Climate adaptation and mitigation is one of CGIAR’s five impact areas, and climate work runs through many of CGIAR’s science programs and accelerators besides Climate Action.

The Climate Action Science Program

Climate Action is CGIAR’s science program on climate for 2025 to 2030, with its own targets and a list of the countries where it expects to bring benefits (both below). It describes its aim as follows:

“We aim to drive science, innovation, and collaboration to transform food, land, and water systems for a climate-resilient, net-zero, and equitable future. Through more resilient, low-emissions farms, landscapes, and aquatic systems, we will advance research across agri-food systems benefiting 16 million vulnerable small-scale producers and value chain actors across 20 countries.”

Source: CGIAR Climate Action
